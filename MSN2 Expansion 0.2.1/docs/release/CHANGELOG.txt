- New: Settings > Controls is rebindable (click a key, press the new one), also from the main menu; conflicts swap, "Reset all keys" restores defaults
- New: Controls page also lists the selfie camera key; HUD key hints (Q, Tab, E, R, F) follow your keys
- New: [Fixes] WardrobeTween, TheftSpots, KeyRemapping options; bindings saved in BepInEx\config\senjuwoo.msn2.bindings.json
